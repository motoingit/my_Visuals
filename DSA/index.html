AVL tree rotations

Pick a case, then step through the rebalancing. BF (balance factor) = height(left) − height(right).

Triangles are whole subtrees (taller = higher). The red dashed triangle is the subtree that just grew after an insertion. Red BF means the AVL rule (|BF| ≤ 1) is broken. Letters z, y, x name the three nodes on the heavy path, from the unbalanced node downward.
